How C5 decides

After the last close of each month it checks two signals: is SPY above its 10-month average, and is the corporate credit spread at or below its 126-day average? Both healthy: 2x the S&P 500. One: the S&P 500. Neither: T-bills. The trade happens at the next day's closing auction.

Trend catches valuation busts like 2000–02; credit catches credit crises like 1990, 1998 and 2008. Neither alone caught both.
