# C5 holdout smoke test (2026-01-02 to 2026-05-19): pre-registration

**Status: LOCKED at this document's first commit.** Written 2026-10-18, before any 2026 data is fetched for this study and before any holdout result exists, on top of `master` commit `8e0e4e4` (branch `research/c5-holdout` was created from it). `CLAUDE.md` rules 1–10 apply. No Azure resource, no Alpaca account, nothing in `src/`.

Nothing below may change after the first real run. A change means a new pre-registration. A dated note headed "Post-run clarification, no rule change" may be appended below; it is not part of the locked rule text.

## 1. The rules (Jorge's, word for word)

- **Rules:** identical to C5 in `docs/specs/Core_Candidates_Preregistration.md`. No parameter changes.
- **Window:** 2026-01-02 to 2026-05-19, the reserved holdout. Lookbacks may use 2025 data. **Read nothing dated after 2026-05-19.** The 2026 refusal in `research/core_candidates` gets an explicit, test-covered allowance for exactly 2026-01-01 to 2026-05-19 in this run only. `backtest.data`'s research guard stays untouched.
- **Purpose: a smoke test, not a verdict.** The window holds about four monthly decisions, too few to judge performance, so **there is no performance gate.** It passes ("SMOKE PASS") if: the data loads with no gaps beyond those counted; every decision is reproduced by an independent verifier; and positions change only at month-end decisions, exactly as the rule states. Performance vs. SPY total return is **reported** (return, max drawdown, decisions and holdings), never judged.

## 2. Interpretations (locked with the rest)

1. **Rules.** C5 is `research.core_candidates.engine` `DEFAULTS["C5"]`, unchanged: 10-month trend test on SPY's adjusted close, `BAA10Y` strictly-prior-day credit value vs its 126-trading-day average, both healthy → simulated 2x S&P, exactly one → SPY, neither → T-bills; financing T-bills + 0.5%, 0.91%/yr expense, 2 bps per dollar traded, next-close execution.
2. **Window and decisions.** Trading days are SPY's yfinance rows. The first decision is the 2025-12-31 month-end, executing at the close of 2026-01-02, when $100,000 is first invested; then the January, February, March and April 2026 month-ends (executing on the next trading day). The May 2026 month-end falls after 2026-05-19 and is not taken. The window ends at the 2026-05-19 close. As in the out-of-sample study, the engine's `FIRST_DECISION` and `SUB_PERIODS` constants are set inside a scoped override (to 2025-12-31 and the single window period), restored on exit; no engine function's behaviour changes.
3. **Data.** `yfinance` SPY daily history from 2024-06-01 with `end` exclusive at 2026-05-20; FRED CSV `DTB3` and `BAA10Y` with `cosd=2024-06-01&coed=2026-05-19`. Cached under `data/reference/c5_holdout/` (gitignored), separate from every other cache. Any row dated after 2026-05-19, received or cached, is a failure. The FRED CSV is parsed by this study's own small parser, because `backtest.wheel_data.parse_fred_csv` refuses 2026 and must stay unchanged.
4. **The allowance.** A single scoped context, `research.core_candidates.holdout.allow(date(2026, 5, 19))`, lets the `research/core_candidates` refusals (`engine.refuse_holdout`, `data.assert_not_holdout`, the cache writer and reader) accept dates from 2026-01-01 through 2026-05-19 while it is active, and only then. It accepts no other end date. Outside it, every 2026 date is refused exactly as before. A test covers all three cases (inside, after 2026-05-19, outside the context).
5. **Smoke criteria, mechanical.** (a) **Data:** every window day has a SPY close and a previous-day T-bill rate, and every carry-forward of `DTB3`/`BAA10Y` is counted and listed (more than 7 calendar days stops the run). (b) **Reproduction:** a separate verifier, importing nothing from `research/core_candidates`, re-derives from the raw cached files every decision in the window (5) and the holding it maps to, and C5's return over each holding period; holdings must match exactly and returns to 1e-9 relative. (c) **Timing:** the holding changes only on a day that is the execution day of a month-end decision (the first trading day after a month-end), never on any other day. All three hold → **SMOKE PASS**; otherwise **SMOKE FAIL**, with the failing check named.
6. **Reported, never judged:** C5's return and maximum drawdown over the window, SPY's total return and maximum drawdown over the same days, each decision's inputs (SPY close, 10-month average, credit value, 126-day average) and holding.
7. Outputs go to `reports/c5_holdout/<run-id>/` (gitignored) and `.review/c5-holdout-results.md`.
